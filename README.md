<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-10.svg" alt="WHAT IS GHOSTVPN?" height="41"></p>

<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-11.svg" alt="GhostVPN hides your real IP address." height="40"></p>

<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-12.svg" alt="It connects you to a VPN server or proxy of your choice, and websites see the server's IP instead of yours." height="60"></p>

<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-13.svg" alt="You keep your own VPN account (NordVPN, Surfshark, ProtonVPN, Mullvad and many more) or use free servers. GhostVPN replaces all their separate apps with one window: pick a country, click CONNECT VPN, done." height="110"></p>

<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-14.svg" alt="QUICK START" height="41"></p>

<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-15.svg" alt="1. Download GhostVPN_portable.zip (button above) and unzip it, e.g. to C:\GhostVPN. 2. Run URUCHOM_GHOST.bat and click Yes when Windows asks for administrator rights. 3. Choose a provider in the top-left corner and add servers: SETTINGS → ADVANCED → UPDATE, or SETTINGS → BROWSE CONFIG for your own .ovpn files. 4. Save your VPN login: SETTINGS → VPN ACCOUNT. 5. Pick a country and server, click CONNECT VPN. The map shows your new location." height="260"></p>

<p><img src="docs/images/g/en-22.svg" alt="Feature What it does | OpenVPN Connects with .ovpn profiles from 19 VPN providers, without their apps. UDP or TCP, automatic UDP → TCP fallback. | WireGuard Fast modern tunnel from your .conf file. The program checks the handshake, so you know the tunnel really works. | Kill Switch Uses Windows Firewall to block all traffic outside the VPN. Tabs for P2P, streaming and gaming. | Secure DNS Sets Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard or OpenDNS for IPv4 and IPv6, and flushes the DNS cache. | Leak tests DNS leak test and WebRTC test: check that nothing gives away your real IP. | Proxy SOCKS5 and HTTP proxy with login, a local bridge, and Firefox launched through the proxy. 19 premium proxy providers. | Proxy tester Multi-threaded tester for proxy lists (.txt) with live results: alive or dead. | Shadowsocks Encrypted tunnel that is hard to block, with ss:// link support and a guide for your own server. | Cloud Sync Downloads fresh server lists of NordVPN, PrivadoVPN, PIA, Mullvad, AirVPN and others in one click. | Route map Shows on the map which countries and nodes your traffic passes through. | Speedtest and data counter Live download/upload charts, speedtest and how much data the session used. | Skins and tray 20 colour themes, Polish and English, works from the tray next to the clock. | Free VPN Ready entries for VPN Gate and IPSpeed public servers." height="965"></p>
